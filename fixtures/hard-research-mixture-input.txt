2. Algorithm
Algorithm 1: Adaptive Newton-MCMC Solver
Input: Initial parameter \( \theta_0 \), tolerance \( \varepsilon \), maximum iterations \( K_{\max} \)
For k = 0, 1, 2, ..., Kmax:
1. Solve forward PDE: \( \mathcal{R}(u_k;\theta_k)=0 \)
2. Compute gradient: \( g_k = \nabla_\theta J(\theta_k) \)
3. If \( \|g_k\|_2 < \varepsilon \), stop.
